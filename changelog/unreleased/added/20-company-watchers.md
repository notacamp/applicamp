- **Watch the companies whose jobs keep fitting you.** Applicamp suggests a company when two of its jobs
  matched you in the last 8 weeks (or you shortlisted one), with every reason tracing back to the postings it
  comes from; nothing is watched until you say so. When you press Watch, it finds the company's own job board
  (the board a posting came from, or the careers page on its site where robots.txt allows: Greenhouse, Lever,
  Ashby or JobPosting data), or you paste the careers page yourself. Then the nightly search also reads that
  board, compares it with yesterday's and scores only the new jobs that pass your requirements: a match lands
  in your Pipeline as Found and Today says "Company posted a job that fits you". Suggesting, finding a board
  and reading it cost nothing; most days there is nothing new, so no AI call at all. Up to 20 companies, on
  the new Companies page.
