- **A stronger profile, step by step.** Profile & goals shows how strong your profile is
  (headline, summary, results on your recent roles, skills, education, languages, a link,
  goals) and what would raise it. "Improve my profile" has the AI read your profile once
  and ask the few questions that fill its gaps, one at a time and always skippable. Your
  answer becomes a draft that may only say what you wrote or your profile already says; you
  keep, edit or discard it, and nothing is saved before you do. "Skills found in your
  roles" lists skills your positions mention but your skills list doesn't, with Add or Not
  mine, and you can reorder skills: the first three drive the job board searches. Today
  shows the strength and a Continue button until the profile is strong, and Not now hides
  it for a week.
