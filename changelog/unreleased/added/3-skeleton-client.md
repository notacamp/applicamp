- **The Applicamp web app.** Sign in with Campbooks or LinkedIn and land on Today, in a calm,
  Notion-like app with a sidebar for Today, Pipeline, Agents and Profile & goals. Each place
  says what will appear there; ⌘K jumps anywhere and `?` lists every shortcut. In English,
  Portuguese, Spanish and French, light or dark. The image serves it, so one container is
  the whole app.
