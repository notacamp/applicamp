- **Grill me: a deep interview about each role.** From Profile & goals, pick a role and
  answer one question at a time, in your language, through seven areas: context, your part,
  stack and scale, results, hard problems, people and stories. Vague answers get pushed back
  on, numbers get asked about, and answers that disagree with each other or your profile get
  asked about. Each answer yields short facts shown with your exact words, and you keep, fix
  or drop each one; nothing is saved without a click, and a fact that says something your
  words and profile don't is left out. Kept facts sit under the role on your profile with
  Remove and Undo. One small AI call per question inside your usual spending limits, no
  background calls, and at most 150 a day.
