  and job reads the company's own website (the posting, its home page and a few pages it links
  to about the company, only where its robots.txt allows, and no other site) and lists what it
  says, each fact with the page's own words and a link to the page; a fact the page doesn't
  back up is left out, and you can remove any that is wrong. It also shows what the job wants
  most, quoting the posting, and up to three questions where your profile is thin: answers
  become facts on a position, kept with the same Keep, Fix and Drop as Grill me, and "I don't
  have it" keeps a skill off your CVs. One AI call to read a company (about EUR 0.002, reused
  for 30 days), one to compare the job, one small one per answer; at most 20 company reads a
  day, and nothing runs until you click.
