- **A tailored CV for each job, every line traced to your profile.** From Prepare, "Draft the
  CV" writes a one-page CV for the job from your profile, the company's facts and what the job
  wants (one AI call, about EUR 0.002; the AI never sees your name or contact details). Every
  line says which part of your profile it comes from, and a check that uses no AI blocks any
  line that says more than its sources: a number, a tool or a name your profile doesn't have,
  a fact about the company passed off as your achievement, a skill you don't list. For each
  blocked line you can edit it, say it is true in your own words (it becomes a fact on that
  role of your profile) or remove it, and Approve stays off until none is blocked. You can see
  what changed from your profile, put back anything the draft left out, and how many of the
  job's keywords are on the CV (keywords you don't have are never added). At most 10 drafts
  per job and 20 a day, and nothing runs until you click. The PDF comes next.
