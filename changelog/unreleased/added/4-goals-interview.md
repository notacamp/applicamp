- **Goals, one question at a time.** After you import your profile, Applicamp offers a short
  interview about what you want next: roles, levels, how and where you work, moving, your cash
  floor (in any ISO 4217 currency) and whether equity counts, the kind of company, what to avoid
  and what you need, the languages you work in, and how much agents may do alone. One question
  per screen, every one skippable; Enter goes to the next, Shift+Enter adds another item to a
  list, and Esc or "Finish later" leaves with what you answered saved. Nothing in the app waits
  for it. Your answers appear under Goals on Profile & goals, each edited in place; every change
  is a new version, so agents can record which one they used (`GET` and `PUT /api/v1/goals`).
