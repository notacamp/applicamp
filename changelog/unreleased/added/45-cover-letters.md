- **A cover letter for each job, every sentence traced to your profile.** Once a job's CV is approved,
  "Write the cover letter" writes a short letter next to it (about 180 words, or about 300) from your
  profile, the facts read on the company's own site and the approved CV, so the two agree (one AI call,
  about EUR 0.002; the AI never sees your name or contact details, and is told to skip "I am writing to
  apply", flattery and clichés). A check that uses no AI flags any sentence that says more than its sources,
  praise nobody wrote ("go-to", "best"), and a sentence about you that rests only on a fact about the company.
  Each paragraph says where it came from (Company fact, From your profile, No claims); nothing is dropped, and
  you fix it with "It's true" (your words join your profile), Edit or Remove. The job's own application
  questions are answered too when your profile can say; ones it can't (a notice period, a salary) come back
  empty for you to answer, as text to copy. Approve makes a one-page PDF in the same style as your CV, read
  back to make sure every paragraph, your name and your email survived. Letters sit on the CVs page (All, CVs,
  Letters, Drafts), in the job's peek and beside the CV review.
