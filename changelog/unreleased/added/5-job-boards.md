- **Searches read remote job boards, and Applicamp keeps finding new ones.** A search now reads
  the job boards the instance's owner approved, remote jobs anywhere as long as they're open to
  where you live: Himalayas, Jobicy, Remote OK, Arbeitnow (remote jobs only) and HN "Who is
  hiring", each within its terms (how often it may be read, a followed link back crediting it,
  its apply link unchanged, expired jobs taken out of view). The same job on several boards is
  one opportunity that names every board, and the side peek shows "From Himalayas" with a link
  to the posting there. Jobs whose board says they're open only to other countries never reach
  the AI. A weekly discovery checks new boards from a public list (their robots rules, the
  sentences of their terms about automated access and credit, a feed and sample jobs) without
  any AI, and they wait on the new Boards page until the owner approves them; you can suggest a
  board too. Boards are ranked by the average salary of their engineering jobs open to you
  (a country's board with few salaries by that country's typical pay), and the Scanned tab
  shows what each search read on each board, how many passed your requirements, were scored by
  AI and matched, and what the AI cost. The hand-picked company boards are switched off in
  searches (`APPLICAMP_SOURCES__COMPANY_BOARDS`), kept for company watchers.
