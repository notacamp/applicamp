- **Your pipeline, and a first job search with spending limits.** Pipeline holds every
  opportunity in one Notion-style database: a table ("All opportunities") and a board by stage
  (Found, Shortlisted, Applying, Applied, Talking, Interview, Offer, Closed), each opening in a
  side peek with why it matches, its gaps, the posting, notes, the next step and "Not for me".
  Paste any job's link to add it: a Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby posting is read from the board,
  any other site is kept as a link you name. "Search now" (on Pipeline, on Today and in ⌘K)
  reads about a hundred companies' public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards, Arbeitnow and
  HN "Who is hiring" (never LinkedIn or Indeed), drops jobs that break your goals, scores the
  best 40 against your profile and goals, and adds the good matches as Found; a search also
  runs every evening when there is something new. Today now shows your next step: set up the
  profile, answer the goals, search, or review new matches. Every AI call is metered in euros
  and refused before it would cross a limit: €0.30 a day and €3 a month per person, €15 a month
  for the instance (`APPLICAMP_AI__BUDGET__…`); you see an estimate before a search and "AI this
  month" on Profile & goals, and `applicamp ai usage` shows the instance's total.
