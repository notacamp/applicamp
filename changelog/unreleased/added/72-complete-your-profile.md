- **Complete your profile: the facts the app already knows, to keep or drop.** The app gathers what
  it learned about your work and does not have on your profile yet. That includes what you told
  Tell me about you, the guesses you confirmed, what your own website says, and achievements a CV
  you imported again lists. You do not have to type any of it again. A new page, "Complete your
  profile", shows each fact under its job, with where it comes from and the words it comes from.
  Each fact has **Keep** and **Drop**, plus **Keep all**. Kept and dropped facts stay on the page
  and can be undone for a day, and a Keep all is undone in one go. What you take back in Tell me
  about you stops waiting, and a fact you dropped is never saved there. Profile & goals and Tell me
  about you say when new facts are waiting. Nothing reaches your profile until you keep it, and no
  AI is used for this.
