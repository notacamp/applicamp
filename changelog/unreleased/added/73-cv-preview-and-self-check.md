- **Every CV draft is a PDF you can see, and the app checks its own lines first.** A drafted CV now opens as
  its PDF right away, and the PDF is made again after every change you make. It fits itself on one page,
  else two. You can switch to Compact or two pages and download the draft. Ordinary words such as
  "Developer" or the city on your profile are no longer flagged; a rank such as "Senior" or "Lead" passes
  only when one of your job titles says it. When a line says more than your profile does, the app rewrites
  it to what your profile says and marks it "Fixed by the app". When it can't, it asks you one question:
  "Is this true?" with Yes, keep it, Change it or Remove it, and shows the exact sentence a yes adds to
  your profile. Approving keeps the PDF you saw, and you can undo it for a few seconds; the cover letter
  waits until then. Nothing is sent to anyone.
