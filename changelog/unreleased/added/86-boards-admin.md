- **A Job boards page for admins.** Board owners see every board on one page: how many are being read, which wait for them and why, what is being checked, what is not allowed, and what happened this week. Each board opens a side panel with the decision first. For a board that wants permission, Applicamp drafts the email to copy and send from your own mailbox, remembers that you sent it, and approves or declines the board when you record their answer.
