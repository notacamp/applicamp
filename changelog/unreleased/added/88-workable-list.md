- **The Workable list adds every Workable company that is hiring, once a day.** The app reads Workable's own list of every company's jobs (about 860 MB) as it downloads, within Workable's conditions and robots rules, and adds each company with a job posted in the last 60 days to the registry as a company page, approved by the app together with Workable (which still waits for your choice while it asks AI tools not to learn from its pages). Only how many jobs each company has is kept; no model is called. A list of boards Workable already integrates with can be excluded, and the download's size and time limits are set by the host.
