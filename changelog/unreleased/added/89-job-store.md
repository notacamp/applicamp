- **A job store: every approved site is read on its own schedule.** Each approved job board and company page is read without waiting for a search, within its own limits (its polling interval, its fetches a day, a request at a time on a host, asking first whether anything changed, a company page once a day, a quiet site less and less often), and what it lists is kept: new or changed jobs only, jobs the site drops closed and deleted after 14 days, expired ones dropped. No model is called. Searches still read the boards live; reading the store comes next. Board owners can see the totals at `GET /api/v1/boards/store`.
