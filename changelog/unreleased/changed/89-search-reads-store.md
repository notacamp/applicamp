- **Searches read the job store, company pages included, and pick the closest jobs without a model.** A search now takes its jobs from the store that is filled on each site's own schedule, including every approved company page, and no longer reads the sites itself; only your own roles and skills queries still go to the sites that can search. Before any AI is used, the jobs are ranked by how close their title, company and tags are to your roles and skills, and only the closest 400 get a quick look, so tens of thousands of stored jobs stay within your daily limit (a night with 1,500 new jobs costs about EUR 0.03). The Searches tab says how many sites and company pages it read from and counts the jobs left for later as "Not among the closest to your roles today". The nightly search looks only at jobs the store first saw since your last search. `APPLICAMP_SOURCES__QUICK_LOOK_PER_SEARCH` (400) is new and `APPLICAMP_SOURCES__SEARCH_COMPANY_PAGES` is gone.
