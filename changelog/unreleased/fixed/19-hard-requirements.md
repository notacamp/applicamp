  whether it is remote, whether someone where you live can take it, the languages it needs and
  the pay it states, and code decides: remote only means remote and open to where you live; a
  language you didn't list, or pay under your floor, is out; a fact the posting doesn't state
  counts as not met. Such a job is never added, whatever its score, and the search line says how
  many were dropped. Matches found before this are checked once and the ones that fail move to
  "Hidden: don't meet your requirements" with the reason (nothing is deleted). Ashby and Lever
  jobs their board marks on site or hybrid are dropped before any model call.
