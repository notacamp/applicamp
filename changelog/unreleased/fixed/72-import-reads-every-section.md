- **A LinkedIn PDF or a CV is read section by section, and a place is never a job's summary.** The
  import used to put the city line under each job's dates where the job's description goes, and
  could come back without dates, achievements, education, languages or a headline. Now the place
  goes to the job's location, the dates and the bullet points are read, and the parts that come
  back empty although the file has their heading are read once more. The review says what was
  found ("Found 6 jobs, 18 achievements, 9 skills, 2 degrees and 2 languages.") and what was not,
  and says so when your AI limit stopped the second read. Merging a new read repairs the jobs an
  earlier import got wrong: a summary that was only a place gives way to the job's description,
  and what you wrote or kept stays. Two jobs with the same title at one company stay two. A PDF
  picked where the LinkedIn export is offered is read as a PDF. If your profile came from a PDF
  before this, upload it again once and choose Merge.
