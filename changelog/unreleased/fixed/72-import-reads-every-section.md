- **A LinkedIn PDF or a CV is read section by section, and a place is never a job's summary.** The
  import used to put the city line under each job's dates ("Lisbon, Portugal") where the job's
  description goes, and could come back without dates, achievements, education or languages. Now the
  place goes to the job's location, the dates and the bullet points are read, and a section that
  comes back empty although the file has its heading is read once more. The review says what was
  found ("Found 13 jobs, 31 achievements, 14 skills, 1 degree and 3 languages.") and what was not.
  Merging a new read repairs the jobs an earlier import got wrong and keeps everything you edited or
  kept. A PDF picked where the LinkedIn export is offered is read as a PDF. If your profile came from
  a PDF before this, upload it again once and choose Merge.
