# Design

The `profile` module owns the data (schema `profile`). The questions live in the wizard's existing pending row (`profile.about_you`), so they last seven days, are swept with the rest and are deleted at Save. Every endpoint needs a signed-in session (no scope: it is the person's own profile) and works only on that account's rows. Errors are problem+json. No events are published or consumed: Save publishes `profile.updated` and `profile.goals_updated` as in part 1.

## Data
- `profile.about_you.questions` (jsonb, default `{}`): `answered` and `skipped` (gap ids), `current` (the question on screen: its gap, question, why, tag, choices), `enough` and `updated` (the change ids the last answer proposed). It is reset whenever the portrait is made again, so a skip lasts for one profile version.
- `profile.about_calls.kind` also allows `question` and `answer`; the daily cap counts them. The cap goes from 30 to 60 (a full run is 39 calls).
- A pending change carries `origin` (the gap it answers; none for "Anything else"), so a new "Anything else" replaces only its own changes and keeps the answers'. Answer changes have ids `q1`, `q2`, …; those of "Anything else" `c1`, …
- The strength counts a kept fact in the `results` area like a highlight (a result is 6 of 8 points of a recent role, a number 2 more). Without it "→ N% if you answer" would promise points Save never gives.

## The gaps (`features/about/gaps.rs`): plain code, no model
`gaps(inputs)` is pure: the profile, the goals, the portrait, the choices, the pending changes, the "Is this accurate?" answer and the skills the person said are not theirs. Each gap is `{id, kind, position?, area?, basis, names}`; `basis` is English, plain, and is data for the model. Ids are stable for a profile version (`assumed:s3`, `results:0`, `team:0`, `area:1:stories`, `skills`, `skills_found`, `claim:elixir`, `summary`, `goals`…). Rank, ascending: assumptions left for later (portrait order), wrong ones after "Not really", results of the latest role, empty skills, team size of a lead, results of the other recent roles, technologies in the roles that are not skills, a told skill no role mentions, summary, headline, background, goals, then the areas of each recent position (three) in order. What the person has told so far (pending role facts, skills, a goal) closes the gap it answers; a kept fact does too. Context is not an area that is ever asked. Results are asked as "a result with a number" (its own gap) rather than as an area.

Two places read words rather than structure, because the brief asks for them: a **lead or manager title** (`gaps::leads`: the title split into words and compared with a short list in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French), and the **technology names** in a role (`kernel::tech`, the same name list the rest of the app uses). Neither reads email or the person's free text.

The limit is 12 questions; "Yes, mostly" lowers it to 8 and "Not really" raises it to 16 and also asks about the assumptions they marked wrong. "Some of it's wrong" is 12.

## The question call (`prompt.rs`, `questions.rs`)
`complete` role, temperature 0, strict schema `{question, why, tag, choices}`, budgets 4000 and one retry at 8000 (the same `ask` as the portrait). The user message holds the language, today's date, the gap, **that position's entry and facts** (title, company, dates, summary, up to 8 highlights, up to 10 facts; no location) or, for a gap about no position, the positions' titles and dates, and the related assumption. No other position's words and no name, email, phone, address or link; a test asserts it on the built message and on the request that reached the fake model. The code (`judge_question`) tidies the output: a `why` the model left out is the gap's own basis (never a question without a reason), an assumption is always tagged I assumed and a claim from "Anything else" You told me, choices are at most six and unique. The instruction forbids asking for anything the model knows or that can be looked up, and tells it to state what it knows as an assumption in `why`.

## The answer call
The same role, schema (`extra_schema`) and checks as "Anything else": `judge_changes` against the person's words (what they typed and the choices they picked, which must be among those offered). A quote that is not theirs and any number, name or tool the value adds are flags, shown, never dropped; settling a flag is part 1's `PUT /about/changes/{id}`. The changes are fitted to the gap (a role fact belongs to the position asked about, in the area asked about, except for an assumption, whose topic is a guess), merged into the pending changes (a newer single-valued field replaces an older one, a repeat is not added), and the answered gap is recorded. The model reads the question only to understand a short answer; it is never evidence.

## Endpoints (`/api/v1/profile/about/questions…`, session, OpenAPI)
`POST /questions/next?locale` (returns the question on screen with no call, or words the top gap with 1 call; with nothing to ask, no call and `done`) · `POST /questions/{id}/answer?locale {text, choices}` (1 call) · `POST /questions/{id}/skip` (no call) · `POST /questions/enough` (no call). All return the wizard's `AboutView`, which gains `questions` (`current`, `answered`, `skipped`, `total`, `left`, `done`, `strength`, `updated`). No portrait for this profile version: 409 `portrait_needed`. Over the cap: 429 `about_daily_cap` before any call. A spending limit is the gateway's, never retried.

## AI use
Role `complete`. Without it the questions say AI is not available and the person goes to the review; every other part of the app works. The person can skip any question, stop at any point, and nothing is saved until Save.

## Retiring Improve, Skills and Grill me
Client: the routes `/profile/improve`, `/profile/skills`, `/profile/grill` and `/profile/grill/$position` redirect to `/profile/about`; their pages, hooks and tests are deleted, as are the Grill me button, the per-role links, the Today nudge's Improve and Skills links (they go to Tell me about you) and the checklist's Add and Improve buttons (they go to Tell me about you; an Add on a part with a place on the page still edits it in place). The strength card no longer asks for the Improve review, so the app no longer starts that background call. The skills list on the profile gets move up and down, so ordering survives the Skills page.

Server: **not deleted, deprecated.** Grill me's proposals (`/profile/grill/proposals/…`) are still how the tailored CV turns an answer about a job into facts (`JobAnswerFacts`, #37), and the Improve review, drafts and suggested skills share tables and a background job with the strength. Taking them out is a migration and a pipeline change that does not belong in this PR; a follow-up issue tracks removing the interview, draft and suggestion endpoints and their tables. They are no longer called by the client and the spec no longer promises them.
