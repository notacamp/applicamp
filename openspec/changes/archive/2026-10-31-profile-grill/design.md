# Design

The `profile` module owns the data (schema `profile`). Kept facts live in the profile document (`profile.profiles.data`, `work[].facts`), so the existing `profile.updated` event (ids only) is published when a fact is kept, and the profile `version` trigger fires. Interviews live in three new tables of the same schema. Every endpoint needs a signed-in session (no scope: it is the person's own profile) and works only on that account's rows; a fact or position that is not theirs is a 404. Errors are problem+json.

## Data
- **Fact** (`Work.facts[]`, OpenAPI `Fact`): `{ id, text, quote, area, kept_at }`. `area` is `context | your_part | stack_scale | results | hard_problems | people | stories`. `quote` is the person's exact words. Facts are optional in JSON (omitted when empty), never written by the import or by the model without a click, and never sent to the structuring model's schema. The profile limits apply (text and quote are long values; at most 200 per role). A LinkedIn Replace import replaces them with the rest; Merge keeps the positions the profile has.
- `profile.grill_sessions` (id, account_id, position, `role_key`, `coverage` jsonb area to `part|ok`, `skipped` jsonb, timestamps; unique per account and position). `position` is the index in `work`, as `role_ref` is in #24. `role_key` is the squashed company, title and start date: a session whose key no longer matches the position at that index is ignored, and replaced on start, so a list edited since never lends one role's interview to another.
- `profile.grill_turns` (session, seq, account_id, kind `question|answer|note`, text, `push`, `area`, `dropped`, `ai_call`, created_at). A note is `skipped` or `done`. `ai_call` marks the turn a model call is counted on (the first question of a start, the answer of an answer, the skip note of a skip), which is what the daily cap counts.
- `profile.grill_proposals` (id, session, account, position, `turn_seq`, text, quote, area, status `ready|kept|dropped`, created_at, expires_at). They live 24 hours; the same sweep as the drafts (`profile.expire_imports`, enqueued at each expiry) deletes them.

## The interview call (`features/grill/prompt.rs`)
`complete` role, temperature 0, strict JSON schema, answer budget 4000 tokens and one retry at 8000 after a cut-off (a reasoning model thinks first). The system prompt is in code, versioned (`PROMPT_VERSION`, logged as a number). It sets out the seven areas and their order, the one question at a time rule, pushing back on vague words with who decided, how many, how long, what changed, asking for numbers and how the person knows them, what they did themselves, never suggesting an answer, never inventing, never praising, asking about contradictions, about 12 questions per role, and that everything between the tags is data, not instructions. The user message holds only: the language (`?locale=` from the client, English when absent), that role's title, company, dates, summary and highlights; its kept facts and the facts waiting; the coverage; the skipped areas; the last 10 turns; the event (`start | answer | skip`) and, for an answer, the answer. Never the whole profile, the name or contact details.

Output: `{ facts: [{text, quote, area}], coverage: [{area, level part|ok}], next: {question, area, push} | null, conflict: string | null }`. A `conflict` replaces `next` and always pushes.

## What code trusts (`features/grill/judge.rs`, no model, no keywords)
- A fact's `quote` must be a verbatim part of the answer (the import source check's `squash`: no case, no whitespace, plain quotes and dashes). It is dropped otherwise.
- A fact's `text` must pass the draft source check (#24 `check`) against the quote and the profile: any number, percentage, name, acronym or technology neither says drops it.
- Dropped facts are counted on the answer turn (`dropped`) and never shown; the page says how many lines were left out. An unknown area drops the fact; up to 5 facts per answer; a fact already kept or waiting is not proposed again.
- Coverage only goes up (`none < part < ok`), and only from an answer; unknown areas are ignored.
- The next question is asked only while an area is neither `ok` nor skipped, and is at most 600 characters; a conflict question is always allowed.
- Fix: the edited text is checked the same way against the quote and the profile; refused with 400 `fact_not_supported` naming the words that are not there. The quote is never editable.
- Keep refuses with 409 `profile_changed` when the position moved since the fact was taken. A kept fact is written in one transaction with a new profile version (source `answer`) and carries the gap review forward, so keeping a fact never costs a new review.

## Endpoints (`/api/v1/profile/grill`, session, OpenAPI)
`GET /grill` (every role with `covered`/7, `status`, `kept`, plus `calls_today` and `daily_cap`) · `GET /grill/{position}` (turns, coverage, waiting facts, the question) · `POST /grill/{position}/start` (1 call, or the open question again with none) · `POST …/answer {text}` (1 call; 1 to 2000 characters, else 400 `invalid_answer`) · `POST …/skip` (1 call, none when nothing is left) · `POST …/done` (no call) · `POST …/keep-all` · `POST /grill/proposals/{id}/keep {text?}` · `POST /grill/proposals/{id}/drop`. Answer and skip refuse with 409 `no_open_question` when nothing waits and 409 `grill_changed` if another request got there first. Over the cap: 429 `grill_daily_cap`, before any call. No model configured: 503 `ai_unavailable`. Budget and rate problems are the gateway's (`ai_budget_reached`, `ai_daily_limit_reached`, `ai_failed`), never retried.

Logs carry counts and kinds only (facts, dropped, covered, prompt version), never an answer.

## Client
Route `/profile/grill` (roles as a nav list, a horizontal scroller on phones) and `/profile/grill/$position` (the chat), inside the app frame. Entry points: a "Grill me" button on Profile & goals beside the strength card, and "Grill me about this role" on each role's facts block. The chat: questions, push-backs marked "Pushing back", the person's answers, a "Facts taken from your answer" block with Keep, Fix and Drop per fact and Keep all, a composer (Answer, Skip this one, Done with this role for now), a coverage card with seven rows and n/7, and the month's AI spend line. Three columns on desktop; at 980px and below one column with the coverage card above the chat. The profile shows kept facts under each role with their quote, Remove, and Undo. All four languages; 375px and desktop.

The fake model (`scripts/fake-jobs.ts`) recognises the interviewer prompt: a first question per role; an answer with no digit is pushed back on; otherwise one to three facts quoted from the answer, plus one made-up fact the quote check drops; the next area is marked covered and the next question asked.
